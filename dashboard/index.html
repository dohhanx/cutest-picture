Pre-token launch radar

Implied value = TVL × category mcap/TVL × 7-day growth × months with no token recorded.

Priced tokens in this snapshot are the comps. CEX, bridge and liquid-staking categories stay out of the multiples.

How a row gets its number

implied = round(TVL × category multiple × growth × seasoning). The same product is what impliedOf recomputes in the page, and it has to match the board.

The category multiple is the 10% trimmed median of live-token market cap divided by TVL, for priced peers in that category. Each ratio is capped at 5× the global 90th percentile first, so one float on a thin TVL cannot print a multiple in the hundreds. The global median clamps ratios to the 10th–90th percentile, then uses the same trimmed median.

Fewer than five peers and the median shrinks: (n/5) × category + (1 − n/5) × global. No peers uses the global median alone. growth = 1 + 0.35 × clamp(change_7d, −40, 80) / 100, and a missing change counts as 0. seasoning = 0.82 + 0.36 × min(months, 30) / 30, with months = age_days / 30.4375 and an unknown listing treated as 12 months. The low/high band widens when the category is thin or a figure is missing.

CEX, Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Cross Chain Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking and Restaked BTC are left out of the comps and out of the pre-token board, along with versioned, wrapped, staked and exchange-prefixed names. The watchlist is still covered in full. Early means above the floor and not on the watchlist. No protocol name is special-cased.

Farm plans

Capital steps with TVL: $40, $80, $150, $300, then $500. Gas is a fee table for the protocol's first chain (Ethereum $14, Base $0.28, Solana $0.15, Polygon $0.18, and the rest of the table; $1 when the chain is not listed). Hold length comes from the category, plus 14 days when the listing is under 120 days old. Each plan has six steps — fund, open, a points action on a fixed cadence, hold, claim, exit — and every step states a dollar cost and a time.

Launch curve

Every protocol that records a token. Families that share a symbol or a CoinGecko id are one comp, so a market cap is not fit twice. Exchanges, bridges and liquid staking are listed and held out of the line.

Not a launch. Each row is off the board for a reason taken from its category or its name: an exchange, a bridge, liquid staking, a wrapped or staked product, a versioned listing, or a kin of a token that already trades.

How a launch is priced

value = 10^a × TVL^b × category × seasoning × momentum × breadth × kin. impliedOf multiplies those stored factors and has to match the board.

a and b come from a log-log fit of family market cap on family TVL. Listings that share a symbol or a CoinGecko id are one family and their TVL is summed, so Aave-style versions are one point. Exchanges, bridges and liquid staking are not on the line. The category factor is 10 to the median log residual, shrunk by n/5 toward 1 when fewer than five families sit in the fit.

seasoning = 0.85 + 0.30 × min(months, 36) / 36. momentum = 1 + 0.5 × clamp(change_7d, −40, 40) / 100. breadth = 1 + 3% × min(chains, 10). kin = 0.5 when the first word matches a protocol that already records a token. The quartile band is the fit's residual interquartile range, widened when the category is thin.

A farm ticket assumes 5% of the implied value is the drop, spread across TVL. Worth it means that drop clears the plan's gas by $6 or more per $1,000. Thin means it clears gas. Skip means gas eats it. The plan is seven steps: fund, open, a points cadence, hold, claim, an exit rule, withdraw.

Leave-one-out refits the line without each family and scores the absolute percent error. A second check predicts the held-out exchange, bridge and staking families with the same line.

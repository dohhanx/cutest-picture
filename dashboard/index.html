Launch curve

Every protocol that records a token. Families that share a symbol or a CoinGecko id are one comp, so a market cap is not fit twice. Exchanges, bridges and liquid staking are listed and held out of the line.

Not a launch. Each row is off the board for a reason taken from its category or its name: an exchange, a bridge, liquid staking, a wrapped or staked product, a versioned listing, or a kin of a token that already trades.

How a launch is priced

The method is a robust log-log regression with category effects. impliedOf multiplies the stored factors and has to match the board. Listings that share a symbol or a CoinGecko id are one family and their TVL is summed. The target is recorded market cap.

A TVL-only line on every eligible family is the baseline. The pricing line drops families whose log10(mcap/TVL) is more than 2 MAD from the median, then estimates log10(mcap) = a + b·log10(TVL) + c·momentum + d·log10(1+months) + a category effect. Momentum is the seven-day change clamped at ±40 points. A category is estimated when five or more families remain; thinner ones shrink toward the reference category by n/5. breadth = 1 + 3% × min(chains, 10). kin = 0.5 when the first word matches a tokened protocol. Breadth and kin sit outside the R².

Worth it means the drop, net of gas and a 4% yield given up, is at least $4 per $1,000 of the minimum ticket. Thin means that net is positive. Skip means it is not. The drop assumes 5% of implied value is shared in proportion to TVL. Seven steps: fund, open, points, hold, claim, an exit rule, withdraw.

What this does not claim

It does not say a token will be issued, or when. The snapshot has no FDV and no TVL path, only the seven-day change. A higher R² means the line fits tokens that already trade. It is not a forecast. The band is the market's own scatter.
